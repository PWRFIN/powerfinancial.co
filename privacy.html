By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information directly from you, automatically through your use of our Services, or from third parties.

You may provide personal information when you:

  • Submit a contact or consultation request;
  • Request information about life insurance, health insurance, annuities, financial products, or related services;
  • Complete a quote, questionnaire, survey, or lead form;
  • Schedule an appointment;
  • Communicate with us by email, telephone, text message, or other means;
  • Apply to become an agent or representative;
  • Submit information through an employment, contracting, onboarding, or business-related form;
  • Subscribe to communications or marketing materials; or
  • Otherwise voluntarily provide information to us.

Depending on the service or interaction, this information may include:

  • Name;
  • Mailing address;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Date of birth;
  • State of residence;
  • Employment or business information;
  • Insurance-related information;
  • Financial information;
  • Information concerning your insurance needs and preferences;
  • Information you voluntarily provide concerning your health or other circumstances relevant to an insurance inquiry or application; and
  • Other information you choose to provide.

You should not submit sensitive personal information through a general website contact form unless specifically requested and the form is intended to collect that information.

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:
