Common Questions

Questions you probably have, answered honestly.

Is the free consultation actually free? +
Completely. No credit card, no obligation. It’s a conversation, nothing more unless you choose to move forward.
Do I need a lot of money to work with you? +
Not at all. We work with people at every stage, whether you’re just starting out or building on what you already have.
What makes you different from a bank or big firm? +
We’re independent. We work for you, not for any single carrier or company. That means we find the best solution for your situation, not for our quota.
Are your insurance agents licensed? +
Yes. Insurance recommendations are made by appropriately state-licensed Life & Health insurance agents operating through the Experior Financial partner network. Investment, tax, and legal services, when needed, are provided separately by appropriately qualified third-party professionals.
How quickly can I get started? +
Many plans can be in place within days of your consultation. We’ll walk you through your exact timeline from the first call.
